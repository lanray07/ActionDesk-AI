# ActionDesk AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

ActionDesk AI is designed to keep your life-admin information under your control.

## Data handling

The current App Store build stores imported documents, extracted details, reminders, drafts and preferences locally on your device. The preview intelligence included in this build runs locally and deterministically. ActionDesk AI does not sell personal information, use it for advertising or track you across apps and websites.

Apple services may process purchase, crash or diagnostic information under Apple's own terms when you use the App Store, TestFlight or in-app purchases. ActionDesk AI does not receive your full payment-card details.

## Permissions

ActionDesk AI may request access to the camera, photo library, files, microphone, speech recognition and notifications only when you use a feature that needs that permission. You can change permissions in iOS Settings.

## Your choices

You can review and delete locally stored items in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to device backups managed by you or Apple.

## Children

ActionDesk AI is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the service changes. Questions or privacy requests can be submitted through the repository's support page:
