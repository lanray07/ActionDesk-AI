## Description

YOUR PAPERWORK HAS NEXT STEPS.
ACTIONDESK FINDS THEM.

ActionDesk AI turns bills, letters, receipts, renewals, contracts and everyday paperwork into clear actions.

Scan or import a document and ActionDesk can identify important dates, amounts, deadlines and potential next steps — helping you understand what requires your attention.

SCAN & UNDERSTAND

Bring in a photo, screenshot, PDF or pasted text. ActionDesk separates details confirmed from the document from AI interpretation, and clearly flags anything missing or unclear.

NEVER MISS A DEADLINE

Spot payment dates, renewals, appointments, return windows and warranty deadlines. Choose when to be reminded; ActionDesk never schedules a consequential action without your confirmation.

TURN PAPERWORK INTO ACTIONS

Every item answers one practical question: “What do I need to do?” Complete steps, add a reminder, mark work as waiting, or resolve it when you are done.

TRACK RENEWALS

Keep insurance, subscriptions and other renewals visible before they become urgent. Compare saved information when previous documents are available.

DRAFT RESPONSES

Create editable enquiries, cancellation requests, billing questions and follow-ups. ActionDesk never sends a message automatically — you stay in control.

ASK ACTIONDESK

Ask what a letter means, what is due this week or what you are waiting for. Get answers grounded in the information you have saved.

USE YOUR VOICE

Speak naturally, review the recognized text, then confirm before reminders or status changes are made.

FIND ANYTHING

Search in everyday language for bills, unresolved tasks, upcoming renewals, warranties and more.

PRIVACY & CONTROL

Use device authentication, protected local storage and controls to export or delete your data. ActionDesk explains whenever a feature requires external processing. AI output can be wrong, so verify important financial, legal, contractual and health information against the original document.

Some features require ActionDesk Pro. Subscription availability and pricing appear before purchase. Terms and privacy policy apply.
